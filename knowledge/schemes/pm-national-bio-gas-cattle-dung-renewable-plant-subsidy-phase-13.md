# PM National Bio-Gas & Cattle Dung Renewable Plant Subsidy Phase 13

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Financial support for installing domestic Gobar-Dhan biogas units. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Bio-Gas & Cattle Dung Renewable Plant Subsidy:** Financial support for installing domestic Gobar-Dhan biogas units. (₹18,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq Farmer`
- `annual_income lte 400000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Electricity / Property Bill** (Mandatory): National Electricity / Property Bill for verification
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): National Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://www.myscheme.gov.in](https://www.myscheme.gov.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
