# Delhi Phase-2 Bio-Gas & Cattle Dung Renewable Plant Subsidy

## 1. Overview & Objective
Financial support for installing domestic Gobar-Dhan biogas units. Implemented by Delhi government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Bio-Gas & Cattle Dung Renewable Plant Subsidy:** Financial support for installing domestic Gobar-Dhan biogas units. (₹18,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Delhi`
- `occupation eq Farmer`
- `annual_income lte 400000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Electricity / Property Bill** (Mandatory): Official Electricity / Property Bill for verification
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Official Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/delhi](https://serviceonline.gov.in/delhi).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
