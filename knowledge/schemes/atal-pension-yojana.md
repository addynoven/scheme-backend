# Atal Pension Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Government-backed pension scheme for workers in the unorganized sector providing guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 post age 60.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Guaranteed Monthly Pension Post 60:** Guaranteed lifetime monthly pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 depending on contribution amount.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `age between 18-40`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Identity proof
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Savings bank account with auto-debit facility

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in](https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
