# Chhattisgarh Export Promotion & Quality Certification Subsidy

## 1. Overview & Objective
Reimbursement for ISO, ZED, and international trade mark registration costs. Implemented by Chhattisgarh government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Export Promotion & Quality Certification Subsidy:** Reimbursement for ISO, ZED, and international trade mark registration costs. (₹75,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Chhattisgarh`
- `occupation eq Business`
- `occupation eq Business`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Udyam Registration Certificate** (Mandatory): Official Udyam Registration Certificate for verification
- **PAN Card** (Mandatory): Official PAN Card for verification
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Bank Account Statement** (Mandatory): Official Bank Account Statement for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/chhattisgarh](https://serviceonline.gov.in/chhattisgarh).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
