# Mukhya Mantri Ladli Behna Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Flagship scheme providing ₹1,250 per month (₹15,000/year) direct benefit transfer into bank accounts of resident women in Madhya Pradesh aged 21-60.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹1,250 Monthly Direct Cash Transfer:** ₹15,000 annual direct cash transfer credited on the 10th of every month into Aadhaar-linked accounts.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Madhya Pradesh`
- `gender eq female`
- `age between 21-60`
- `annual_income lte 250000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Samagra Family ID** (Mandatory): MP Samagra Portal 9-digit member ID
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Mandatory identity proof linked with mobile
- **Aadhaar-Linked Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Bank account with active DBT enable status

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://ladlibehna.mp.gov.in](https://ladlibehna.mp.gov.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
