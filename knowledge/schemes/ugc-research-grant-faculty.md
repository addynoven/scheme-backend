# Research Grant For In-Service Faculty Members

## 1. Overview & Objective
The scheme "Research Grant for In-Service Faculty Members" was introduced by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Department of Higher Education (DoHE). The objective of this research grant is to provide opportunities to regularly appointed faculty members of Universities/ Institutes/ Colleges (hereinafter “Institutions”) to pursue research in their area(s).

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **The quantum of support under the scheme is ₹ 10,00,000.:** The quantum of support under the scheme is ₹ 10,00,000.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `age lte 50`
- `occupation in faculty,professor,teacher,academic`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Scanned copy of your passport-size photograph and signature (size upto 1MB, format: jpg)** (Mandatory): Scanned copy of your passport-size photograph and signature (size upto 1MB, format: jpg)
- **Complete Research proposal (size upto 5 MB) and an abstract (size upto 1MB)** (Mandatory): Complete Research proposal (size upto 5 MB) and an abstract (size upto 1MB)
- **Auto-generated form signed by the HoD/Registrar** (Mandatory): Auto-generated form signed by the HoD/Registrar
- **Forwarding letter from the University Registrar** (Mandatory): Forwarding letter from the University Registrar

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://frg.ugc.ac.in/](https://frg.ugc.ac.in/).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
