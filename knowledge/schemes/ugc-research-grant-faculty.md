# Research Grant For In-Service Faculty Members

## 1. Overview & Objectives
The scheme "Research Grant for In-Service Faculty Members" was introduced by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Department of Higher Education (DoHE). The objective of this research grant is to provide opportunities to regularly appointed faculty members of Universities/ Institutes/ Colleges (hereinafter “Institutions”) to pursue research in their area(s).

## 2. Benefits & Support
- **The quantum of support under the scheme is ₹ 10,00,000.:** The quantum of support under the scheme is ₹ 10,00,000.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- `age lte 50`
- `occupation in faculty,professor,teacher,academic`

## 4. Exclusions & Disqualifications
- Termination of Award:
- The Grant is liable to be terminated in the case of:
- Misconduct.
- The awardee is found ineligible later.
- Any false/misleading information furnished by the awardee.
- Plagiarism or unethical practices.
- Any corrupt practices by the awardee.
- Violation of terms and conditions of the fellowship.

## 5. Application Process (Online)
Step 1: Read the Guidelines at https://frg.ugc.ac.in/
Step 2: Registration - click on New User and provide email
Step 3: Login with Scheme Login Credentials from ugchelp@mail.inflibnet.ac.in
Step 4: Dashboard - complete profile and click on Eligibility Criteria
Step 5: Eligibility alert - read criteria
Step 6: Eligibility - submit required details
Step 7: Data Capturing Format - fill in PG and Ph.D. details
Step 8: PG details
Step 9: Ph.D. Details and upload research proposal
Step 10: Declaration and submit application

## 6. Required Documents
