# Andhra Pradesh Phase-2 Sukanya Samriddhi High-Interest Top-Up

## 1. Overview & Objective
State government interest subsidy top-up on girl child savings accounts. Implemented by Andhra Pradesh government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Sukanya Samriddhi High-Interest Top-Up:** State government interest subsidy top-up on girl child savings accounts. (₹10,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Andhra Pradesh`
- `gender eq Female`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Ration Card** (Mandatory): Official Ration Card for verification
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Official Bank Passbook for verification
- **Income Certificate** (Optional): Official Income Certificate for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/andhra-pradesh](https://serviceonline.gov.in/andhra-pradesh).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
