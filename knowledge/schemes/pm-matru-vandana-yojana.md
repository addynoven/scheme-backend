# Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Maternity benefit cash incentive of ₹5,000 provided in installments for pregnant women and lactating mothers for health and nutrition.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹5,000 Direct Maternity Cash Incentive:** Direct cash assistance to compensate for wage loss and support nutritious diet before and after delivery.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `gender eq female`
- `age gte 19`
- `annual_income lte 800000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Identity proof of mother
- **MCP Card** (Mandatory): Mother and Child Protection card
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Bank account in mother's name

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in](https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
