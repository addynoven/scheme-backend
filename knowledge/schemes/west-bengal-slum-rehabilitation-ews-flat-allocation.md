# West Bengal Slum Rehabilitation & EWS Flat Allocation

## 1. Overview & Objective
Subsidized ownership allotment of 1BHK flats with basic civic amenities. Implemented by West Bengal government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Slum Rehabilitation & EWS Flat Allocation:** Subsidized ownership allotment of 1BHK flats with basic civic amenities. (₹250,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq West Bengal`
- `annual_income lte 200000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Land Possession Certificate** (Mandatory): Official Land Possession Certificate for verification
- **Income Certificate** (Mandatory): Official Income Certificate for verification
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Official Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/west-bengal](https://serviceonline.gov.in/west-bengal).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
