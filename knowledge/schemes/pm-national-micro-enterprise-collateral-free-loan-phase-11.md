# PM National Micro Enterprise Collateral-Free Loan Phase 11

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Guaranteed working capital and equipment loan up to ₹10 Lakh at subsidized interest. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Micro Enterprise Collateral-Free Loan:** Guaranteed working capital and equipment loan up to ₹10 Lakh at subsidized interest. (₹200,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `occupation eq Business`
- `annual_income lte 800000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Udyam Registration Certificate** (Mandatory): National Udyam Registration Certificate for verification
- **PAN Card** (Mandatory): National PAN Card for verification
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Bank Account Statement** (Mandatory): National Bank Account Statement for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://www.myscheme.gov.in](https://www.myscheme.gov.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
