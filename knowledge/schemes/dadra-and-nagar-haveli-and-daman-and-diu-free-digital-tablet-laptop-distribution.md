# Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu Free Digital Tablet & Laptop Distribution

## 1. Overview & Objective
High-speed digital learning devices distributed to meritorious board students. Implemented by Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu government for citizen welfare.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **Free Digital Tablet & Laptop Distribution:** High-speed digital learning devices distributed to meritorious board students. (₹15,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu`
- `occupation eq Student`
- `annual_income lte 400000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Official Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Marksheet / Grade Certificate** (Mandatory): Official Marksheet / Grade Certificate for verification
- **Income Certificate** (Mandatory): Official Income Certificate for verification
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Official Bank Passbook for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://serviceonline.gov.in/dadra-and-nagar-haveli-and-daman-and-diu](https://serviceonline.gov.in/dadra-and-nagar-haveli-and-daman-and-diu).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
