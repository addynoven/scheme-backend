# PM National Universal Cashless Health Insurance Phase 3

## 1. Overview & Objective
Central flagship program: Annual cashless hospitalization and surgical treatment cover in network hospitals. Administered by Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **National Universal Cashless Health Insurance:** Annual cashless hospitalization and surgical treatment cover in network hospitals. (₹500,000)

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `annual_income lte 500000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): National Aadhaar Card for verification
- **Ration Card / Health Card** (Mandatory): National Ration Card / Health Card for verification
- **Medical Prescription** (Mandatory): National Medical Prescription for verification

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://www.myscheme.gov.in](https://www.myscheme.gov.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
