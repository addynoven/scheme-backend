# Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
Maharashtra state cash grant of ₹6,000 per year in three installments of ₹2,000, complementing PM-Kisan for total ₹12,000 farmer income support.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹6,000 Annual Maharashtra Farmer Grant:** ₹2,000 per installment transferred directly to verified landholding farmers in Maharashtra.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Maharashtra`
- `occupation eq farmer`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **7/12 Land Extract** (Mandatory): Official land ownership record from Maharashtra Revenue Department
- **8-A Holding Extract** (Mandatory): Khata details of agricultural land
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Identity proof
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Aadhaar-seeded bank account

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in](https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
