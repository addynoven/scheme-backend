# Mukhya Mantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana

## 1. Overview & Objective
State financial assistance of ₹6,000 per year paid in three installments of ₹2,000, complementing PM-Kisan to provide a total ₹12,000 annual farmer support.

## 2. Benefits & Financial Assistance
- **₹6,000 Annual MP State Farmer Grant:** ₹2,000 transferred in three installments directly to verified landholding farmers in MP.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria (Deterministic Rules)
- `state eq Madhya Pradesh`
- `occupation eq farmer`
- `annual_income lte 300000`

## 4. Required Documents Checklist
- **Khasra Land Ownership Record** (Mandatory): Official agricultural land record from Patwari / SAARA
- **Aadhaar Card** (Mandatory): Identity proof
- **Bank Passbook** (Mandatory): Aadhaar-seeded bank account
- **PM Kisan Registration ID** (Mandatory): Active PM-Kisan beneficiary registration number

## 5. Application Procedure
1. Verify that your citizen profile satisfies the eligibility rules listed above.
2. Ensure you have the required documents uploaded and verified in your Document Vault.
3. Access the official application portal: [https://saara.mp.gov.in](https://saara.mp.gov.in).
4. Submit the verified application with your registration number.
